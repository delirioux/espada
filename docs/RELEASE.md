Espada 0.1: the first version of a generative groovebox for the M-VAVE FM-1 in the style of early-90s PC tracker music.

- Five lanes (lead, pad, bass, drums, sfx) with FM voices, a synthesized drum kit and noise effects; notes scroll past a fixed play line. HOME steps from the overview to each lane on its own.
- Five profiles: EPIC, HYPERSPACE, FUSION, OLDSKOOL, SYMPHONY. Queue, blend lane by lane, launch on the phrase, lock a lane.
- Four knobs a channel: MELODY, RHYTHM, PITCH and CHANGE. The lead plays 16-bar arcs of two 8-bar periods, closed on the root.
- Instruments including piano, strings, violins, cellos, brass and a chaos instrument; stereo voices and a room (SPACE).
- Density from sparse to busy, inside the chord and its scale; ENERGY for all lanes at once.
- Per lane: filter with a tempo-synced wobble, FM brightness, drive, crush, level; slide, retrigger, echo; VARY changes the character per phrase.
- ENV fades a lane out and in; the MIX page has the levels.
- Your own parts on the EDIT page, entered in scale steps so they follow the chords; GENERATE and GRAB.
- The SONG page: key, scale, suggested progressions, bars per chord.
- The arc (off until you turn it on): builds from the drums, a break, the drop, and again; DROP by hand (tap or hold).
- USB-MIDI out (lead, pad and bass on channels 1 to 3, drums on 10), MIDI start / stop in.
- Playable in the browser: the same code, compiled to WebAssembly.

Install it over M-VAVE's official firmware (V15). Hold OCT- while switching on for the USB rescue.
